## Closing entries

These cells are residuals that make an account close, not observed flows:

- (row,cap) = EUR +1.1m (+0.000% of GDP)
- (row,cap) closes the capital factor account (cap): gross operating surplus and mixed income from the supply-use tables, less the amount the sector accounts distribute to domestic sectors. It measures the discrepancy between the two sources (see Cross-source consistency) and is booked against the rest of the world, so it also appears in the row residual. If negative, balancing flips it to (cap,row), where it reads as factor income from abroad; the balanced CSV's note column marks it.
